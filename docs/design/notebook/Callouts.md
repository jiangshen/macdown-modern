# A little context, right where you need it

Callouts give a useful detail a quiet place on the page.

> [!NOTE]
> Keep the idea in **plain Markdown**. 日本語 · 中文 · 👩‍💻

> [!TIP]
> Use a short note to help your future self.
>
> - Keep it specific.
> - Make the next step clear.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your document stays a normal Markdown file. No special format is required.

> [!WARNING]
> Check the details before making a change.

> [!CAUTION]
> Keep a copy of anything you cannot replace.
